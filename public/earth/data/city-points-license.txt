City lights for the globe

Placement, light intensity, individual dusk timing and source mesh design:
Geographical Adventures, Sebastian Lague
https://github.com/SebLague/Geographical-Adventures/tree/82fcda20bebb033c749b2339e9ce3a6e58007699/Assets/Scripts/Game/City%20Lights

Original 151,601-point asset:
https://github.com/SebLague/Geographical-Adventures/blob/82fcda20bebb033c749b2339e9ce3a6e58007699/Assets/Data/City%20Lights/CityLightPoints.json
The source project credits NASA Earth Observatory's Night Lights:
https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights

Local adaptation: geographic coordinates, spatial ordering and a binary
representation for the browser, WGS84 placement, orbital display scale and
twilight range. These are sampled lights from a satellite brightness map.
