## About me

I grew up in Kentucky and got into programming by making Roblox games. The studio I co-founded went on to reach 20 million plays. After I graduate from the University of Michigan in May 2027, I’m joining Roblox as a software engineer.

### Graphics

I like figuring out how to draw things that move. In my [ocean simulation](/posts/ocean-simulation), that meant waves. At Michigan’s Field Robotics Group, it meant simulating the echoes a sonar picks up from the seafloor.
