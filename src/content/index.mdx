# Tanner Aslan

Hey, I'm Tanner Aslan.

I'm a senior studying a B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Michigan Ann Arbor, but I'm originally from Kentucky. I graduate in May 2027.

I started my CS journey with game development, but I'm now interested in full stack development and graphics programming.

This last summer, I was a front-end software engineering intern at **Klaviyo** in Boston, where I was on the activation team. The summer before that, I was on the checkout team at **SeatGeek** in NYC.

Before Klaviyo, I spent a few months as a research assistant with the **University of Michigan Field Robotics Group**, building a GPU-accelerated side-scan sonar simulator in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. That work became **"OceanSim 2.0,"** a paper accepted to IEEE OCEANS 2026.

I like building things and exploring ways to integrate WebGL into website design. Right now I'm working on **[shadercn.com](https://shadercn.com)**, an open-source React component library for adding shaders to your website, and **[MaizeMeals](https://www.maizemeals.com/)**, a dining app for UofM students.

Outside of work, I play the violin and enjoy composing music as a hobby.

This portfolio is built with **Next.js**, **React**, and **Typescript**. It's deployed on **Vercel** and the source code is available on **[Github](https://github.com/tanneraslan/website)**.
